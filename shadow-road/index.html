Shadow Road

Cast out of the true city, the fallen Prince of Shadow walks the road home through its shadow worlds: blooming valleys, clockwork skies, prism storms. You set out with bare fists. Take weapons from those who carry them, break the guardians, and forge your arsenal between worlds.

MOVE & RUNJoystick to walk. Flick it twice in a direction to run.
MIX YOUR COMBOSAttack and Heavy chain together: try Attack, Attack, Heavy. Each weapon has its own combos.
LAUNCH & JUGGLEHold Attack to launch an enemy, tap Jump to follow them up, then Attack up to three times in the air.
DASH ATTACKSAttack or Heavy while running.
DODGE & COUNTERDodge in any direction; nothing can hit you mid-dash. Tap Attack during it to strike back.
WEAPONSDefeated enemies sometimes drop a new weapon. Tap Swap to change weapons.
ARMORED FOESEnemies glowing gold shrug off light hits. Break their guard with Heavy attacks or launchers, then they're open. They often drop sigils.
CRITICAL HITSHit enemies from behind, or while they're staggered, knocked down or airborne, for a much better chance of a critical.
TRUMP CARDSBuy them at the forge with sigils. Land hits to charge your card, then tap Trump to play it.

Best played with your phone held sideways.
