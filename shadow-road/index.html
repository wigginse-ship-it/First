Shadow Road

Every world between here and the true city is only its shadow: blooming valleys, clockwork skies, prism storms. You set out with bare fists. Break open weapon shrines, beat the guardians, and forge your arsenal between worlds.

MOVE & RUNJoystick to walk. Flick it twice in a direction to run. On a keyboard: arrows, double-tap to run.
MIX YOUR COMBOSAttack and Heavy chain together: try Attack, Attack, Heavy. Each weapon has its own routes. J and K.
DASH ATTACKSAttack or Heavy while running.
AIR ATTACKSJump, then Attack. Space.
DODGEA roll you can't be hit during. Cancels most attacks. L.
WEAPONSGlowing shrines hold weapons. Smash them. Swap with Q.
TRUMP CARDSInscribe them at the forge with sigils. Land hits to charge one, then play it. E.
