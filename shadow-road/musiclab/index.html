Piano roll

Add: shift-click or double-click empty space (drag right to set the length), or turn on Draw mode and just click-drag. Move: drag a note. Resize: drag the handle on its right edge. Delete: right-click, Alt-click, or select it and press Delete. Arrow keys nudge the selected note (Shift for an octave). Dragging empty space scrolls the roll, and clicking the bar numbers plays from that bar. Notes stay inside their bar. Chords: add a note on top of an existing one and it goes to the second voice (two can sound at once); the second voice is the track's harm line.

Editing on the roll rewrites the lead (or harm) bars here as L(24, 'F5:2 G5:2 ...') lines, one note:length pair per note. L builds one bar from note:length pairs; a quarter note is 6 steps, an eighth 3, a triplet eighth 2.
